Privacy policy

Last updated — 08/12/26 Effective — 08/12/26 Applies to — sonderence.com

This site is informational. There are no accounts to create, no forms to fill in, and no database here holding visitor information. The only things collected are anonymous analytics about which pages get read, and whatever you choose to submit to our scheduling provider when you book a call.

In short
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we never have.
  • There are no logins and no customer database on this site.
  • Analytics record which pages are viewed and roughly what kind of device and country they were viewed from. They are anonymous and cookieless.
  • Booking a call takes you to Calendly, and the details you enter there are sent to us through their service.
01

Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Sonderence website and to information we receive when you contact us or book a call through it.

Sonderence is the trade name of a sole proprietorship owned and operated by [Full legal name], with its principal place of business in Sacramento, California. We are responsible for the information described here.

Privacy questions and requests go to elly@sonderence.com.

This policy does not cover what third-party services do on their own systems. Where those services are involved, we name them in section 06 and link to their policies.

02

Information we collect

Information you give us. The site itself has no contact form, no newsletter signup, no account creation, no downloads, and takes no payments. There are exactly two ways information reaches us:

  • Booking a call. What you type into Calendly — typically your name, email address, chosen time, and anything you add in the booking notes.
  • Emailing us. Your email address, your name if you include it, and whatever you write.

Information collected automatically. When you load a page, our host and analytics receive standard request details: IP address, browser type and version, operating system, device type, approximate location at the country level, the referring page, and the date and time. This is ordinary web infrastructure data, generated by your browser rather than entered by you.

We do not collect Social Security numbers, financial account details, precise geolocation, biometric data, health information, or any other category treated as sensitive under state privacy law.

03

Analytics and behavioural data

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics. It measures which pages are viewed and how often, the referring source, the country a visit came from, and the browser, operating system and device type used. It also records page load performance.

It does not track clicks, scroll depth, mouse movement, or session recordings. It does not follow you from page to page as an identified individual, does not build a profile, and does not track you across other websites. The reporting we see is aggregate — counts and percentages, not people. Cloudflare samples a portion of page load events rather than measuring every one, so even those counts are estimates.

Cloudflare retains this analytics data for a limited rolling window — currently around 30 days — after which it is no longer available to us. [Confirm the current window in your Cloudflare dashboard before publishing.]

04

Cookies and similar technologies

This site sets no cookies. Cloudflare Web Analytics is cookieless: it does not write cookies, does not use localStorage or sessionStorage, and does not fingerprint your device. There are no advertising pixels, no tracking scripts, and no analytics cookies.

Because there is nothing to consent to, there is no cookie banner.

Cloudflare may set a cookie at the network level to distinguish genuine visitors from automated traffic where security measures are triggered. That is a security function, not tracking, and it is not used for advertising.

If you follow a link away from this site — to Calendly, or to a social profile — that site may set its own cookies under its own policy. You can clear or block cookies at any time in your browser settings.

05

How we use the information

We use what we receive to:

  • Respond to your enquiry
  • Schedule, prepare for, and hold a conversation with you
  • Keep a record of who we have spoken with and about what
  • Understand in aggregate which pages are useful, so the site can be improved
  • Keep the site available and protect it against abuse and attacks
  • Carry out client work, where an enquiry becomes an engagement
  • Meet legal, tax, and record-keeping obligations

We do not send marketing emails, because we do not run a mailing list. We do not build advertising profiles, and we do not make automated decisions about you.

07

Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

We disclose information only in these narrow cases:

  • To the providers listed above, so they can perform their function on our behalf
  • Where required by law, legal process, or a valid government request
  • To protect our legal rights, safety, or property, or to investigate fraud or a security issue
  • To a successor, if the business is ever sold or transferred — in which case this policy would continue to apply until you were notified of any change
08

Retention

Enquiry emails and booking records. Kept for as long as they are useful to the conversation they relate to, and generally no longer than two years from our last contact with you. At the end of that period they are deleted from our inbox and removed from our CRM.

Client records. Where an enquiry becomes an engagement, project records are kept for the duration of the work and afterwards as needed for tax and legal record-keeping.

Analytics. Retained by Cloudflare on its own schedule, described in section 03, and then gone. We keep no separate copy.

You can ask us to delete your information sooner. See section 09.

09

Your choices and rights

Access and deletion. Email elly@sonderence.com to ask what we hold about you, get a copy of it, have it corrected, or have it deleted. We usually respond within 30 days. We may need to confirm your identity first, and we will not treat you any differently for asking.

Opting out of analytics. There is no opt-out toggle, because Cloudflare Web Analytics does not identify you — there is no individual record to opt out of. If you would still rather not be counted, any standard content blocker will stop the analytics script from loading, and the site will work exactly as before.

Global Privacy Control. We honour GPC and similar browser opt-out signals. Since we do not sell or share personal information and use no tracking cookies, the signal has nothing to switch off here — but if that ever changes, it will be respected.

Email. We do not run a mailing list, so there is nothing to unsubscribe from.

10

State privacy rights

Several states — including California, Colorado, Virginia, Connecticut and Texas — give residents rights over their personal information. These laws apply to businesses above certain revenue or data-volume thresholds. Sonderence is a sole proprietorship and does not meet those thresholds, so these obligations are not mandatory for us. We extend the following rights to everyone anyway, whatever state you live in.

Categories we collect. In the language these laws use, we collect:

  • Identifiers — name, email address, IP address
  • Internet or network activity — pages viewed, referring page, browser and device type
  • Commercial information — only where an enquiry becomes a client engagement

We collect no other statutory category, and no sensitive personal information as those laws define it.

Sale and sharing. We do not sell personal information. We do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising. We do not use or disclose sensitive personal information for any purpose requiring a right to limit. There is therefore no “Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information” mechanism on this site, because there is nothing to opt out of.

Rights we honour. To know, access, correct, delete, obtain a portable copy, and be free from discrimination for exercising any of them.

How to submit a request. Email elly@sonderence.com with what you are asking for. An authorised agent may submit on your behalf with written proof of authorisation. If we decline a request, we will explain why, and you may reply to that decision at the same address.

11

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. We use reputable providers, protect our accounts with strong authentication, and limit access to information to what is actually needed to do the work.

That said, no method of transmission or storage over the internet is perfectly secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please do not send financial account details, government ID numbers, or health information by email.

12

Children

This site is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13, and if we learn that we have, we will delete it promptly. If you believe a child has provided us with information, email us and we will take care of it.

Our Terms of Service separately ask that the site be used only by people 18 and over.
